## About Me

I work as a Cyber & Digital Risk Associate at PwC India. Outside of my day job, I like building things with code—ranging from native Android utilities and compiler experiments to lightweight web tools.

I didn't take a traditional computer science path, but I enjoy learning how systems work under the hood. Most of my projects start with a simple question: *"How does this actually work, and can I build a tool for it?"*

### What I Work On

- **Day Job:** Cyber risk advisory, internal controls, system validation, and digital resilience.
- **Side Projects:** Android development with Kotlin, CLI utilities, network tools, and client-side web apps.
- **Workflow:** Using modern tools and AI as a coding assistant to research faster, debug bottlenecks, and turn ideas into working software.
