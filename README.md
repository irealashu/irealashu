## About Me

I work as a Cyber & Digital Risk Associate at PwC India. Outside of advisory work, I engineer practical software—ranging from low-level Android utilities and compiler experiments to privacy-focused client-side web tools.

I explore systems from first principles. Most of my projects start with a simple question: *"How does this work under the hood, and can I engineer a cleaner, local-first alternative?"*

- **Advisory & Risk:** Cyber risk assessment, internal controls, computerized system validation (CSV), and digital resilience.
- **Systems & Android:** Terminal utilities, APK packaging, Shizuku ADB privilege integration, and native Kotlin engines.
- **Modern Web:** Local-first, private-by-design applications built with TypeScript, React, Vite, and Tailwind CSS.
